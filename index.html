What I do

Audio plugin
development
services

Discuss a project ↓

Plugin development

DSP, user interfaces and application logic. I can build a product from the first prototype or work alongside your existing team.

Black-box & grey-box modeling

Hardware character captured in software, using measured behavior and circuit knowledge. Models designed for the sound and the real-time budget.

Builds, testing & release

VST3, AU and AAX on macOS and Windows. Build and test automation, signing, installers, copy protection, the whole nine yards.

Support & maintenance

Profiling, optimization and refactoring of existing products. Keeping your shipped plugins alive through host and OS updates.

Supporting software

Web services, telemetry and development tools. New ones, or integrations with what you already run.

About

Hi, I’m Bence

I’m an independent audio software developer based in Denmark, working remotely with teams in Europe and North America.

I work directly with product teams, designers and audio specialists. That can mean taking a new idea through to a finished plugin, or joining an existing codebase to solve a particular problem.
